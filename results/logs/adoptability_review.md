## Demonstrated findings

### 1. Complete the identifying particulars before filing

**Severity: high for sending the current letter unchanged.**

Location: [participation_request_uz.tex:40](</home/xola/GitHub/Solial Registry/docs/participation_request_uz.tex:40>), with the actual placeholders at lines 46, 48 and 74. The letter contains `[F.I.Sh.]`, an unfilled address/contact field and an unfilled signature line. It openly describes itself as an unsent draft.

The petitions law requires identifying particulars and a signature for a written petition; anonymous petitions are not considered. Its Articles 3, 6 and 29 are the relevant provisions. [Official current petitions law](https://lex.uz/docs/-3336169).

**Defensible recipient objection if the unchanged document is sent as a standalone written petition:** the sender has not been identified and the written petition has not been signed. An identifying electronic submission can supply the relevant sender information through its own procedure; this review does not assume that a blank attachment alone satisfies that procedure.

**Smallest remedy:** provide the real name, residential address and signature, or complete the corresponding requirements in the chosen electronic channel. Email and telephone do not replace the required particulars. Do not invent identity information or a signature. This unfinished field does not invalidate the scientific calculations.

### 2. Remove the specific ministerial reference from the operative clause

**Severity: medium for integration into a Cabinet or Presidential act.**

Location: [normative_act_draft_uz.tex:47](</home/xola/GitHub/Solial Registry/docs/normative_act_draft_uz.tex:47>). Operative clause 4 expressly names the methodology registered as No. 3620. It was approved by the Justice Minister's order of 24 April 2025, No. 8-mh. [Official methodology and approving order](https://lex.uz/docs/-7495608).

The statutory unified drafting methodology, paragraph 11, prohibits references to specific lower-ranking normative acts. Article 26 includes reference drafting within legal examination. [O‘RQ-682 and its appended methodology](https://lex.uz/docs/-5378966).

**Defensible integration objection:** the clause cannot be copied unchanged into a higher-ranking instrument without revising this reference. This is a legal-technical defect in the proposed operative text, not a reason that a citizen may not propose it.

**Smallest remedy:** refer generally to applicable scientific-examination legislation in the operative clause. Retain the precise No. 3620 source in the explanatory note and proposed examination terms. The named Cabinet Resolution 35 reference at line 48 must likewise be reconsidered if the developer selects a Presidential instrument; that conditional issue cannot be resolved before the final instrument form is known. An explanatory citation is not the same as an operative incorporation.
