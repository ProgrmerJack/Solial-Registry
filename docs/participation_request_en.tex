\section*{Scientific and technical proposal for implementing PF-193 and participation in a working group}
I present the enclosed author’s proposal on verification of multidimensional assessment calculations, data quality, recording calculation versions and explaining the grounds for decisions, for inclusion in the normative legal act being drafted under paragraphs 13–14 of Presidential Decree No. PF-193 of 11 September 2026.

The substantive part is a proposal to improve state activity within the meaning of Article 3 of the Law “On Appeals of Individuals and Legal Entities”. It contributes to the already mandated PF-193 draft. Article 20 of the Law “On Normative Legal Acts” limits initiating drafts where existing statutory mechanisms and administrative powers suffice. Where a proposed task can lawfully be performed under existing powers, please identify the relevant power and implementation solution; a new act is not requested merely to repeat an existing duty. New external obligations and changes to higher-ranking criteria require the competent legal instrument.

Under Articles 20, 22 and 23 of the Law “On Normative Legal Acts”, I request that you:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Consider the proposed provisions within the draft being prepared under paragraph 14 of PF-193; prioritize the five-paragraph compact alternative or an equivalent normative solution.
\item Consider organizing a scientific and technical discussion with the drafting unit or working group responsible for this subject.
\item Consider including me in the working group on verification of the calculation methodology, on the basis of the work described below and the enclosed reproduction tools. I consent to such participation and am ready to take part in a technical discussion and calculation demonstration for assessment of my relevant knowledge and experience.
\item Consider providing the current draft and dated calculation specification for discussion, identifying the approved act or draft status, units, periods, boundaries, exceptions, rounding and quality rules. Where lawfully disclosable, please identify existing independent expected-answer checks, release/approval records and their coverage of the enclosed cases, so duplicate work can be avoided. For any uncovered task, please identify the responsible unit, existing contractual coverage, incremental work and staff hours, applicable costing provisions and lawful funding. If integration changes application processing, use actual workload and timing evidence; no public counter is offered as a capacity estimate. Family records and protected security particulars are not requested.
\item For each proposal, identify the wording incorporated into the draft, the solution accepted with amendments, the precise basis in current law if the issue is already resolved, or the reason why the proposal was not accepted. For an issue to be resolved in another act, identifying that act and the responsible authority would be useful. This is the requested form of reply to a citizen’s proposal; it does not represent that portal discussion under Article 24 has taken place.
\end{enumerate}
My proposed Article 22 contribution is confined to translating the approved methodology into a reproducible rule description, preparing boundary and exception control cases, checking units and reference periods, and drafting the corresponding verification provisions with the responsible officials. I request routing to the unit preparing the PF-193 draft, with technical participation of the Agency’s Information Technologies Centre where appropriate; Resolution No. 35, annex 1 paragraph 46, assigns the responsible officials of that institution responsibility for lawful and technically correct operation of the registry and its information system. I do not request authority to choose assistance policy or approve eligibility criteria. If working-group appointment is not selected, please consider using the enclosed scientific and technical recommendations in the drafting process under Article 22 and identify the responsible contact for any further demonstration.

The principal verification annex may be considered separately from the optional category-equality and land-formula amendments set out in the explanatory note. Those policy options require the competent legal amendments and an assessment of their effects; they are not conditions for accepting the verification proposal.

Materials 1–2 separate necessary normative content from internal verification. Please prioritize consideration of Material 1's five compact alternative paragraphs in the principal PF-193 draft, or equivalent norms preserving competence, scope and dates. The full edition is a consolidation option, not a cumulative additional demand. For technical tasks, reuse equivalent existing checks; for legal tasks, identify the specific norm securing the same binding consequence. Test coverage does not supply an absent legal criterion. An authoritative input restriction or competent interpretation of an existing rule is accepted where its source, scope and date are identified; interpretation does not introduce a new criterion. Compact normative wording or a cross-reference constitutes substantive adoption. No national manual service, new statistical programme, continuation of the 2026 methodology, transition entitlement or debt exemption is proposed. Incremental hours, contract coverage, capacity and funds remain unverified; only uncovered integration is to be costed.

Please register this submission under Article 23 of the Law “On Appeals of Individuals and Legal Entities” and provide its receipt date and registration number. That article prohibits refusal to register; Article 18 requires acceptance and consideration subject to Articles 29–30. If an issue falls outside the recipient’s powers, please forward it to the competent body within the five-day period and notify me in writing or electronically under the appeals law. Please coordinate issues assigned to several internal units under Article 24. These references concern the \href{https://lex.uz/docs/-3336169}{appeals law}, not the differently numbered provisions of the norm-making law.

Please send a written or electronic reply with specific grounds addressing every issue, under Article 27 of the appeals law. Article 28 provides consideration of the normative proposal within one month of receipt, except where additional study is required, in which case the proposer is notified in writing within ten days. For the personal participation and access requests, please identify the applicable classification and period where they are treated separately; I do not impose the proposal period on a differently classified request. If a proposal or request is not accepted, please explain the applicable review procedure under Article 33. No right to appointment or adoption is asserted.

Article 23 of the \href{https://lex.uz/docs/-5378966}{Law “On Normative Legal Acts”} requires the developer to study relevant scientific research and citizens’ submissions and to generalize and use proposals and scientific recommendations in drafting. Please consider the enclosed findings in that process even if working-group participation is not selected. If I participate in a public or specialist discussion, please acquaint me with the draft in advance under that article, subject to lawful restrictions. This request does not assert unrestricted access to protected information or that portal consultation has already occurred.

I do not request that personal data of real families be sent to me. The calculation tools are proposed for use in an environment controlled by an authorized operator, subject to a lawful basis and anonymisation requirements. Synthetic examples demonstrate software verification; they are not evidence that real families’ categories or needs are correctly identified. The calculation audit of current published rules reproduces the official example in Resolution No. 35 and provides control cases for income exactly equal to minimum consumption expenditure and for negative derived land area. Their occurrence in the operational information system and the number of affected families have not yet been established.

I consent to drafting participation under Article 22. The requested contribution is the preparation of normative wording and reproducible calculation controls.

\section*{Statement of relevant completed work for Article 22}
The project provides a tool using exact rational arithmetic for current published income formulas, a separate arithmetic verification and automated tests. Verification comprises 37 tests; the land formula records 4\,105 constructed cases, including 620 cases with negative derived area. Recalculation of the official example gives 432\,000 soum. These figures are not results from a sample of real families.

The supplied electronic computational supplement contains the execution instructions, exact inputs, expected results, independent coverage checks and outputs underlying these figures. The statement evidences the completed work relevant to Article 22; it does not assert an academic degree or representation of an institution. I am prepared to demonstrate the calculations to the drafting team.
