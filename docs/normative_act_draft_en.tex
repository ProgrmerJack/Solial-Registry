\begin{flushright}Material 1\end{flushright}
\begin{center}\textbf{PROPOSALS}\par
\textbf{for inclusion in the draft normative legal act being prepared to implement paragraph 14 of Decree of the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan No. PF-193 of 11 September 2026}\end{center}

\noindent Submitted by: Ashuraliyev Abduxoliq Akmal ugli. Edition of 8 October 2026. English companion translation; the Russian edition is primary.

Section I is proposed for inclusion in the act being prepared on the new system for including families in the Social Registry through multidimensional assessment. Section II amends the current Regulation on the Procedure for Maintaining the Social Registry, and Section III contains a transitional provision for those amendments. If the act being prepared replaces that Regulation, the rules of Sections II and III are included in the corresponding norms of the new act. The division into sections serves convenience of consideration; the structural units of the act and their numbering are determined by the developer.

\section*{Section I. Correctness and verifiability of multidimensional assessment calculations}

\norm{1}The following basic terms are used in this Section:

multidimensional assessment methodology means the criteria, data sources, scoring rules and threshold values, approved in the prescribed manner, on the basis of which the assessment result is determined;

assessment result means inclusion of a family in the Social Registry with assignment to a category, refusal of inclusion, and a change of the family’s category or its removal from the Social Registry following reassessment;

calculation version means the combination of the edition of the multidimensional assessment methodology, the software version and the parameter values under which an assessment result was determined;

control example means a set of a family’s input data with a score and an assessment result determined in advance under the multidimensional assessment methodology;

control calculation means determination of the score and the assessment result under the multidimensional assessment methodology without using the software being checked;

unconfirmed data means information for which a reply from the information system of a state body or organization has not been received, has been received in an improper format, or conflicts with information from another source.

\norm{2}The responsible staff of the state institution “Information Technologies Centre” under the National Agency for Social Protection under the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan (hereinafter, the Centre and the Agency respectively) shall be responsible for the conformity of the calculations of the “Yagona milliy ijtimoiy himoya” information system (hereinafter, the YAMIH system) to the multidimensional assessment methodology.

\norm{3}The multidimensional assessment methodology shall establish for each criterion:
\sub{(a)}the data source;
\sub{(b)}the unit of measurement;
\sub{(c)}the period for which data are taken into account;
\sub{(d)}the scoring procedure;
\sub{(e)}the rounding procedure;
\sub{(f)}the treatment of the criterion where data are unconfirmed, including the value applied if provided for by legislation;
\sub{(g)}the range of admissible values of the input data and of the calculation result within which a calculated area, quantity or income does not take negative values and counted income does not decrease when actual income increases and the other data are unchanged.

\norm{4}The multidimensional assessment methodology shall determine, for each threshold value, the category to which a family belongs when its score or income equals that value.

\norm{5}The multidimensional assessment methodology shall establish an order of applying exceptions and special procedures under which identical family data correspond to only one assessment result.

\norm{6}The multidimensional assessment methodology shall contain calculation examples for each category and for refusal of inclusion, including cases of equality to a threshold value.

\norm{7}Each edition of the multidimensional assessment methodology shall state:
\sub{(a)}the date from which it applies;
\sub{(b)}for each threshold value, interval, exception and special procedure not established by the methodology itself, the norm of the normative legal act that establishes it.

\norm{8}The assessment result shall be determined in the YAMIH system only under the conditions established by the multidimensional assessment methodology and other normative legal acts.

\norm{9}Non-receipt of a reply to an electronic request of the YAMIH system shall not be treated as absence of the family income or property about which information was requested.

\norm{10}Where data are unconfirmed:
\sub{(a)}the YAMIH system shall mark the relevant criterion;
\sub{(b)}the Centre shall send a repeat request to the relevant information system within one working day;
\sub{(c)}the applicant shall be informed which information requires clarification and in which body or organization;
\sub{(d)}unconfirmed status of data is not in itself a ground for refusal of inclusion, a change of the family’s category or its removal from the Social Registry; such a decision is permitted only on a ground established by the multidimensional assessment methodology or legislation, with that ground indicated in the information provided for in paragraph 17 and disclosed to the applicant under paragraph 18 of this Section;
\sub{(e)}if the information remains unconfirmed ten working days after the initial request or when the decision period expires, whichever is earlier, the assessment is completed by applying the treatment provided for in subpoint (f) of paragraph 3 of this Section; repeat requests do not restart these periods, and non-receipt of a reply does not suspend the decision period;
\sub{(f)}in a reassessment, until it is completed under subpoint (e) of this paragraph, the family’s category is not changed and the family is not removed from the Social Registry on the basis of that reassessment.

\norm{11}The structural unit of the Agency responsible for the multidimensional assessment methodology shall approve a set of control examples including:
\sub{(a)}each threshold value and the values one unit of measurement below and above it;
\sub{(b)}each exception and special procedure provided for by the multidimensional assessment methodology, and their combinations that may apply to one family;
\sub{(c)}each type of unconfirmed data;
\sub{(d)}the calculation examples contained in the multidimensional assessment methodology;
\sub{(e)}each boundary of an income interval established for a category by a normative legal act of higher legal force than the multidimensional assessment methodology, and the values one unit of measurement below and above it;
\sub{(f)}the boundaries of the range of admissible values provided for in subpoint (g) of paragraph 3 of this Section.

\norm{12}The scores and assessment results of control examples shall be determined by control calculation without the participation of the developer of the software being checked, and the assessment results of the control examples provided for in subpoint (e) of paragraph 11 of this Section shall be determined under the normative legal act that establishes the relevant interval.

\norm{13}The Centre shall ensure that the scores and assessment results of the YAMIH system are compared with the scores and results of the control examples before the multidimensional assessment methodology is first applied and before each change to the methodology, the parameter values or the YAMIH system software that affects the score or the assessment result is applied, except in the cases provided for in paragraph 22 of this Section.

\norm{14}The first application of the multidimensional assessment methodology and a change referred to in paragraph 13 of this Section are permitted only where the scores and assessment results of the YAMIH system do not differ from the control examples and all control examples provided for in paragraph 11 of this Section exist; if these conditions are not met by the date of application established by legislation, the transition procedure, ensuring consideration of applications and continued provision of assistance already awarded, is determined by the body that approved the multidimensional assessment methodology.

\norm{15}Comparison results shall be documented in a protocol among the documents provided for by the established procedure for commissioning and changing information systems. The protocol shall state:
\sub{(a)}the calculation version: the edition of the multidimensional assessment methodology, the software version and the parameter values;
\sub{(b)}the number of control examples compared;
\sub{(c)}the differences found in scores and assessment results and the measures to eliminate them;
\sub{(d)}the coverage of each requirement of paragraph 11 of this Section, with the control examples that check it;
\sub{(e)}the surnames and positions of the persons who performed the comparison.

\norm{16}The set of control examples shall be supplemented with each change to the multidimensional assessment methodology; previously approved control examples that meet paragraphs 11 and 12 of this Section shall be reused.

\norm{17}For each assessment result, the YAMIH system shall retain, for the period established by legislation for keeping information on the assessment result:
\sub{(a)}the calculation version;
\sub{(b)}criterion values with their source, period and date of receipt of the information;
\sub{(c)}an unconfirmed-data mark for each criterion;
\sub{(d)}the score and the threshold value applied;
\sub{(e)}the normative incomes applied and the grounds for applying them;
\sub{(f)}access to the edition of the multidimensional assessment methodology, the parameter values and the executable software version that allows the calculation to be performed again;
\sub{(g)}the specific factual and legal grounds for refusal of inclusion, a change of category or removal from the Social Registry, including the provision of the normative legal act applied, if such a decision was taken.

\norm{18}Together with notification of the assessment result, the applicant shall be provided, through the personal account on the Unified Portal of Interactive Public Services and, on the applicant’s request, at an “Inson” social services centre, with information on:
\sub{(a)}the values of the family’s criteria with their sources and periods;
\sub{(b)}the score and the threshold value applied;
\sub{(c)}the normative incomes applied and the grounds for applying them;
\sub{(d)}the information requiring clarification;
\sub{(e)}the possible legal consequences of the decision;
\sub{(f)}the procedure for lodging an objection and protecting the applicant’s rights, and the time limit and procedure for appealing the decision;
\sub{(g)}the specific factual and legal grounds for the decision, including the provision of the normative legal act applied.

\norm{19}In the information provided for in paragraph 18 of this Section, special personal data of other family members shall be indicated only as the fact that the relevant criterion was applied.

\norm{20}The Centre shall check by control calculation a discrepancy between an assessment result already applied to a family and the multidimensional assessment methodology within ten working days of its detection, and a discrepancy stated in an applicant’s objection or complaint within the period for its consideration, and, within ten working days of the day the discrepancy is confirmed, shall identify all assessment results affected by the discrepancy using the information provided for in paragraph 17 of this Section and send their list to the Agency. Until a corrected calculation version meeting paragraphs 13–15 of this Section is applied, the Agency shall, within its powers, take measures excluding application of the confirmed discrepancy to new assessment results.

\norm{21}Within one month of receiving the list provided for in paragraph 20 or 22 of this Section, the Agency shall ensure the review of the assessment results in it in the prescribed manner, under the methodology and legislation applicable to the original assessment and taking transitional provisions into account, and notification of the applicants of the review result through the personal account; the consequences of the review for assistance awarded are determined in accordance with legislation, taking into account the protection of trust in an administrative act. Identification and review of affected results do not extend the time limits established by legislation for considering an applicant’s objection or complaint and notifying its outcome.

\norm{22}A decision to change the algorithm temporarily in cases provided for by legislation, its grounds and period of application shall be recorded in the YAMIH system; assessment results determined under the temporary algorithm shall be marked and, within ten working days after data exchange is restored, checked against a correct implementation of the multidimensional assessment methodology applicable to the original assessment, taking transitional provisions into account; the Centre shall send the list of differing results to the Agency, which shall ensure their review in the manner and within the period provided for in paragraph 21 of this Section.

\norm{23}This Section shall be implemented within the funds provided for maintaining and developing the YAMIH system.

\section*{Section II. Amendments to the Regulation on the Procedure for Maintaining the Social Registry (Annex 1 to Resolution of the Cabinet of Ministers of the Republic of Uzbekistan No. 35 of 29 January 2026)}

The Regulation is officially published in the state language. The exact wording of the amendments in the state language is given in the Uzbek edition of this Material and in the comparison table of Material 2.

\norm{1}In the first paragraph of point 26, replace the words “or the amount of transfers is less than twice the minimum wage” with the words “or the monthly average amount of transfers from a family member working or carrying on entrepreneurial or other activity abroad is less than the normative income established for that member by this point”.

\norm{2}Supplement point 27, after the sixth paragraph, with a paragraph reading:

“If the TEM value determined by the formula is negative, TEM shall be taken as zero.”

\norm{3}In subpoint “a” of point 33, replace the words “higher than minimum consumption expenditure” with the words “equal to or higher than minimum consumption expenditure”.

\norm{4}Supplement point 37 with a second paragraph reading:

“At the same time, through the personal account on the Unified Portal of Interactive Public Services and, on the applicant’s request, at an ‘Inson’ social services centre, the applicant shall be provided with information on the data on which the decision was based, the specific factual and legal grounds for the decision, including the provision of the normative legal act applied, the normative incomes applied, the decision-making procedure, its possible legal consequences, the procedure for lodging an objection and protecting the applicant’s rights, and the time limit and procedure for appealing the decision. Special personal data of other family members shall be indicated only as the fact that the relevant criterion was applied.”

\norm{5}Supplement point 44 with a second paragraph reading:

“In this case the applicant shall be provided with the information provided for in the second paragraph of point 37 of this Regulation, except in the cases provided for in the fifth and sixth paragraphs of point 42 of this Regulation.”
\section*{Section III. Transitional provision}

\norm{1}The amendments provided for in paragraphs 1–3 of Section II apply to applications considered after their entry into force.

\norm{2}For families included in the Social Registry before the amendments enter into force, the amendments apply:
\sub{(a)}to a “state-supported family” and a “poor family” — from the re-completion of the questionnaire under the second paragraph of point 40 of the Regulation;
\sub{(b)}to a “family at the poverty boundary” — from the end of the twelve-month period provided for in the fourth paragraph of point 42 of the Regulation.

\norm{3}Until the dates specified in paragraph 2 of this Section, a change of a family’s category or its removal from the Social Registry is not permitted if it results solely from applying the amendments provided for in paragraphs 1–3 of Section II; the consequences of changes in the family’s composition, income, property and other circumstances are determined in the prescribed manner under the rules in force before the amendments entered into force.
