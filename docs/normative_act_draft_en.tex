\begin{center}\textbf{PROPOSALS}\par
\textbf{for inclusion in the draft normative legal act being prepared to implement paragraph 14 of Decree of the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan No. PF-193 of 11 September 2026}\end{center}

\noindent Submitted by: Abduxoliq Akmal ugli Ashuraliyev. Edition of 3 October 2026. English companion translation; the Russian edition is primary.

Section I is proposed for inclusion in the act being prepared on the new system for including families in the Social Registry through multidimensional assessment. Section II amends the current Regulation on the Procedure for Maintaining the Social Registry. If the act being prepared replaces that Regulation, the rules of Section II are included in the corresponding norms of the new act. Final numbering is determined by the developer.

\section*{Section I. Correctness and verifiability of multidimensional assessment calculations}

\norm{1}The following basic terms are used in this Section:

multidimensional assessment methodology means the criteria, data sources, scoring rules and threshold values, approved in the prescribed manner, on the basis of which the assessment result is determined;

assessment result means inclusion of a family in the Social Registry with assignment to a category, or refusal of inclusion;

calculation version means the combination of the edition of the multidimensional assessment methodology, the software version and the parameter values under which an assessment result was determined;

control example means a set of a family’s input data with an assessment result determined in advance under the multidimensional assessment methodology;

control calculation means determination of the assessment result under the multidimensional assessment methodology without using the software being checked;

unconfirmed data means information for which a reply from the information system of a state body or organization has not been received, has been received in an improper format, or conflicts with information from another source.

\norm{2}The responsible staff of the state institution “Information Technologies Centre” under the National Agency for Social Protection under the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan (hereinafter, the Centre and the Agency respectively) shall be responsible for the conformity of the calculations of the “Yagona milliy ijtimoiy himoya” information system (hereinafter, the YAMIH system) to the multidimensional assessment methodology.

\norm{3}The multidimensional assessment methodology shall establish for each criterion:
\sub{(a)}the data source;
\sub{(b)}the unit of measurement;
\sub{(c)}the period for which data are taken into account;
\sub{(d)}the scoring procedure;
\sub{(e)}the rounding procedure;
\sub{(f)}the value applied where data are unconfirmed, if such application is provided for by legislation.

\norm{4}The multidimensional assessment methodology shall determine, for each threshold value, the category to which a family belongs when its score or income equals that value.

\norm{5}The multidimensional assessment methodology shall establish an order of applying exceptions and special procedures under which identical family data correspond to only one assessment result.

\norm{6}The multidimensional assessment methodology shall contain calculation examples for each category and for refusal of inclusion, including cases of equality to a threshold value.

\norm{7}Each edition of the multidimensional assessment methodology shall state the date from which it applies.

\norm{8}The assessment result shall be determined in the YAMIH system only under the conditions established by the multidimensional assessment methodology and other normative legal acts.

\norm{9}Non-receipt of a reply to an electronic request of the YAMIH system shall not be treated as absence of the family income or property about which information was requested.

\norm{10}Where data are unconfirmed, the YAMIH system shall mark the relevant criterion, and the applicant shall be informed which information requires clarification and in which body or organization.

\norm{11}The structural unit of the Agency responsible for the multidimensional assessment methodology shall approve a set of control examples including:
\sub{(a)}each threshold value and the values one unit of measurement below and above it;
\sub{(b)}each exception and special procedure provided for by the multidimensional assessment methodology;
\sub{(c)}each type of unconfirmed data;
\sub{(d)}the calculation examples contained in the multidimensional assessment methodology.

\norm{12}The results of control examples shall be determined by control calculation without the participation of the developer of the software being checked.

\norm{13}The Centre shall ensure that results of the YAMIH system are compared with the results of the control examples before the multidimensional assessment methodology is first applied and before each change to the methodology or to the YAMIH system software that affects the assessment result is applied, except in the cases provided for in paragraph 22 of this Section.

\norm{14}A change for which the comparison reveals a difference between the assessment result and the result of a control example shall be applied after the difference is eliminated.

\norm{15}Comparison results shall be documented in a protocol among the documents provided for by the established procedure for commissioning and changing information systems. The protocol shall state:
\sub{(a)}the edition of the multidimensional assessment methodology and the software version;
\sub{(b)}the number of control examples compared;
\sub{(c)}the differences found and the measures to eliminate them;
\sub{(d)}the surnames and positions of the persons who performed the comparison.

\norm{16}The set of control examples shall be supplemented with each change to the multidimensional assessment methodology.

\norm{17}For each assessment result, the YAMIH system shall retain:
\sub{(a)}the calculation version;
\sub{(b)}criterion values with their source, period and date of receipt of the information;
\sub{(c)}an unconfirmed-data mark for each criterion;
\sub{(d)}the score and the threshold value applied;
\sub{(e)}the normative incomes applied and the grounds for applying them.

\norm{18}Together with notification of the assessment result, the applicant shall be provided, through the personal account on the Unified Portal of Interactive Public Services and, on the applicant’s request, at an “Inson” social services centre, with information on:
\sub{(a)}the values of the family’s criteria with their sources and periods;
\sub{(b)}the score and the threshold value applied;
\sub{(c)}the normative incomes applied and the grounds for applying them;
\sub{(d)}the information requiring clarification;
\sub{(e)}the possible legal consequences of the decision;
\sub{(f)}the procedure for lodging an objection and protecting the applicant’s rights.

\norm{19}In the information provided for in paragraph 18 of this Section, special personal data of other family members shall be indicated only as the fact that the relevant criterion was applied.

\norm{20}Where an assessment result already applied to a family is found to conflict with the multidimensional assessment methodology, the Centre shall, within ten days, identify all assessment results affected by the conflict using the information provided for in paragraph 17 of this Section and send their list to the Agency.

\norm{21}The Agency shall ensure the review of the assessment results specified in paragraph 20 of this Section in the prescribed manner.

\norm{22}Assessment results determined under an algorithm temporarily changed in cases provided for by legislation shall be marked in the YAMIH system and, after data exchange is restored, reviewed in the prescribed manner.

\norm{23}This Section shall be implemented within the funds provided for maintaining and developing the YAMIH system.

\section*{Section II. Amendments to the Regulation on the Procedure for Maintaining the Social Registry (Annex 1 to Resolution of the Cabinet of Ministers of the Republic of Uzbekistan No. 35 of 29 January 2026)}

The Regulation is officially published in the state language. The exact wording of the amendments in the state language is given in the Uzbek edition of this Material and in the comparison table of Material 2.

\norm{1}In the first paragraph of point 26, replace the words “or the amount of transfers is less than twice the minimum wage” with the words “or the monthly average amount of transfers from a family member abroad is less than the normative income established for that member by this point”.

\norm{2}Supplement point 27, after the sixth paragraph, with a paragraph reading:

“If the TEM value determined by the formula is negative, TEM shall be taken as zero.”

\norm{3}In subpoint “a” of point 33, replace the words “higher than minimum consumption expenditure” with the words “equal to or higher than minimum consumption expenditure”.

\norm{4}Supplement point 37 with a second paragraph reading:

“At the same time, through the personal account on the Unified Portal of Interactive Public Services and, on the applicant’s request, at an ‘Inson’ social services centre, the applicant shall be provided with information on the data on which the decision was based, the normative incomes applied, the decision-making procedure, its possible legal consequences, and the procedure for lodging an objection and protecting the applicant’s rights. Special personal data of other family members shall be indicated only as the fact that the relevant criterion was applied.”
