\begin{center}\textbf{EXPLANATORY NOTE}\end{center}
\noindent Scientific and technical justification of the author’s proposal on verification of Social Registry calculations. Revised 1 October 2026; calculation reference date: 30 September 2026. English companion to the primary Russian submission. Material 2; Material 1 contains the proposed norms and Material 3 the detailed research and electronic computational supplement.\par
\section*{Basis for preparation, developers and purpose}
Paragraphs 13–14 of PF-193 provide for multidimensional assessment from 1 January 2027 and submission of the relevant draft to the Presidential Administration by 1 December 2026. The official developers are the National Agency for Social Protection under the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan and the Ministry of Economy and Finance of the Republic of Uzbekistan. This note accompanies the author’s proposal by Abduxoliq Akmal ugli Ashuraliyev; it is not submitted on behalf of an official developer.

The purpose is to recalculate the approved assessment rule from identical inputs, check boundaries and exceptions, distinguish data problems from software errors, and explain individual decisions. The core proposal consists of seven operative provisions and a 28-paragraph annex. It does not itself set the new 2027 scores or assistance amounts. The expected result is a calculation that can be verified and reconstructed; no quantitative forecast of family welfare, budget savings or assistance coverage is provided. A five-paragraph compact alternative is also supplied to replace the full form, without cumulative application.

The legal form will be determined within the principal draft. An option affecting criteria in PF-258 requires amendment of the Presidential act. A Cabinet of Ministers act or software setting cannot replace a criterion of higher legal force.
\section*{Regulatory subject and structure of the proposed act}
The principal decision is mandatory checking of the execution of approved rules, rather than selection of a new need model. Seven operative provisions cover approval, one methodology description, reproducibility, Article 22 drafting organization, consequential reconciliation, resource accounting and readiness before use. The annex has seven chapters: general provisions (paragraphs 1–3); descriptions and versions (4–7); data quality and outages (8–11); calculation checks (12–17); grounds and review of decisions (18–20); research, reporting and oversight (21–24); acceptance, correction and retention (25–28). Material 1 contains the normative requirements; this note explains purpose and implementation, and Material 3 supplies detailed evidence.

The proposal concerns subordinate implementation of the PF-193 mandate. Paragraph 84’s requirement to examine moving subordinate regulation to statutory level applies when preparing a draft Law; no separate draft Law is proposed here. The competent body chooses the principal instrument. A new entitlement or payment amount requires the competent legal rank. Calculation verification does not replace that authority.

\section*{Necessity of each requirement block}
The existing duties of proper operation and timely provision of information in Resolution No. 35 annex 1 paragraph 46 are the starting point. The proposed procedure makes their execution checkable: a particular version identifies the applied rule, input basis, component comparisons and person resolving a discrepancy. The same paragraph’s duties of the applicant, social worker and “mahalla seven” remain; state-source responsibility is not shifted to the family.

Criterion passports and dated versions are needed because the same number with different units, periods or inclusion rules is not the same input. Separate quality states prevent an absent record becoming confirmed zero income or later receipt becoming presumed greater reliability. Exception and precedence checks prevent a correct general formula from producing a wrong result through omission of a special condition. Component comparisons detect errors cancelling in the total. Individual explanation and linked correction establish the particular decision ground and lawful review, rather than merely statistical reporting.

The Procedure establishes minimum required information and actions. The Agency may define internal passport, protocol and journal forms without changing a criterion, application period or refusal ground. If the developer identifies an equivalent existing requirement and an operationally supported control, the clause is integrated with it. A general reference to an information system or oversight does not resolve demonstrated questions of units, boundaries, exceptions and explanation.

\section*{Necessity of normative content and limits of a technical solution}
\textbf{What must be justified.} Paragraphs 13–14 of \href{https://lex.uz/docs/-8472526}{PF-193} introduce multidimensional point-based classification and mandate a normative draft. They directly support work on the 2027 normative content, but do not establish that a separate testing act or all 28 author clauses are indispensable. The proposal seeks incorporation into the already mandated act; Material 1 supplies a five-paragraph compact alternative. No 2027 scores, weights or complete parameter set have been invented here; the developer must determine them lawfully.

\textbf{Why a technical solution cannot substitute for a legal one.} Articles 3, 14, 17, 18 and 37–40 of \href{https://lex.uz/docs/-5378966}{O‘RQ-682} distinguish normative content, competence, legal hierarchy, required form, registration where applicable and official publication. Where a threshold, equality treatment, period, permissible input or imputation itself establishes an inclusion or refusal condition, a successful test alone cannot justify that choice. Testing asks whether the chosen rule is executed; the competent instrument supplies the legal basis for applying that rule to a family. This is the author's inference from those provisions, not a claim that every software parameter requires separate normative approval. Existing lawful delegation and technical execution of an already determined condition remain.

\textbf{Distinguishing instruments.} Paragraphs 94–95 of the \href{https://lex.uz/docs/-7766987}{Rules registered as No. 3686 on 9 October 2025} cover departmental normative acts affecting rights and safeguards, interagency acts and acts binding organizations outside the adopting body's system. Paragraph 96(b) excludes implementation documents containing no new legal norms; paragraphs 96(v)–(j) provide technical exceptions, including Ministry of Justice classification of other documents. Calling a document a technical requirement therefore does not settle its legal character. Conversely, an actual technical document does not become normative merely because of this proposal. These rules concern the departmental form; they do not require state registration of Presidential or Cabinet acts. The version incorporating the 17 September 2026 amendments was checked; paragraph 20 expressly requires examining necessity and alternatives.

\textbf{Two independent equivalence conditions.} Legal coverage identifies the exact provision, competent body, legal rank, covered families/cases, applicable dates and binding consequence, with applicable registration/publication for a departmental instrument. Technical coverage identifies inputs, dates, exceptions, independently justified expected answers, tested version and executed results. Coverage of all author tests removes the need to repeat those tests. It removes the need for an additional norm only if the legal content is also fully secured. A general contract, audit, group-appointment order or acceptance protocol is not evidence of a missing criterion. Where both coverages are established, no additional norm is proposed for that issue.

\textbf{Applicability restrictions and interpretation.} A competent interpretation of an existing condition or documented source-data domain may resolve a particular counterexample. The source, authority, period, units and actual field semantics are identified; the mathematical conclusion is then restricted to that domain. Current software rejection of a value does not itself prove that a family is legally barred from presenting the corresponding fact. A new eligibility restriction cannot be inserted as purported evidence of an impossible input. Under O‘RQ-682 Article 55, interpretation cannot amend, supplement or correct an act. Clarified meaning requires consistent execution; a new choice of legal condition requires competent amendment. A resolved issue is not retained as an artificial reason for new regulation.

\textbf{An expressly existing temporary power.} Resolution No. 35 annex 1 paragraph 48 covers technical faults, interruptions and incomplete receipt of electronic information. It permits the Agency to suspend exchange in individual systems until full restoration and change the automatic category algorithm. Consequently, this submission does not assert that every temporary algorithm change requires a new act. Compact paragraph 4 and full paragraph 11 preserve that power and propose traceability of its particular exercise. The developer reconciles its application to the new methodology; paragraph 48 is not treated as determination of the entire permanent 2027 scoring system.

\textbf{Cost and choice sequence.} The mandated draft first determines lawful assessment conditions and retained safeguards, alongside comparison of existing tests and contracts. Only uncovered integration is then costed. The compact text creates no new application-processing stream or national manual service. Legal drafting, running tests and interface changes still require time; neither absence of a separate appropriation nor a promise of internal execution proves zero cost. Actual hours, contract coverage, capacity and financing remain unverified. A substantiated resource difficulty may change technical detail and task allocation, but does not legally authorize software to supply a missing assistance condition.

\textbf{Adoption criterion.} Different ordering, shorter wording, a reference to current law or a properly authorized departmental normative act are acceptable. Each legal task is checked for the same binding consequence, source, dates and competence; each technical task for its operator and equivalent evidence. This mapping is requested for discussion, not asserted to be a new mandatory form of governmental reply. Equivalent wording in a competent published act is normative uptake without a separate annex. Incorporating cases only into technical requirements is technical uptake; it does not dispose of demonstrated unresolved normative content.

\section*{Internal technical specification retained outside the normative annex}
This section is the author’s implementation specification for the internal regulation in operative paragraph 4. It is not an additional public criterion, applicant obligation or source of authority to modify law.

\textbf{Criterion and source passports.} Record a stable criterion identifier, legal rule, type and domain, source owner and record identifier, unit, required status, reference-date boundaries, updating rule, transformation, duplicate/overlap treatment, conflict selection, lawful imputation, order of operations and intermediate precision. Remittances and comparison thresholds use the same unit and period; household membership and changing remuneration parameters use their applicable dates. Receipt time is not the fact’s reference date. Preserve both conflicting values and the lawful selection basis. Record a staff correction’s author, time and authority.

\textbf{Control inventory.} Link each applicable condition, boundary, exception, precedence rule and reachable interaction to cases. Each case records its identifier, inputs, preconditions, effective date, legally derived intermediate and final expected values, derivation and independent checker. Compare the planned and executed identifiers and multiplicities; explain exclusions and inapplicability. Check empty inventories and undefined denominators explicitly. A changed rule or source mapping triggers affected and interacting-path checks; if the effect cannot be bounded, check the whole applicable chain. A shared program output is not an independent legal answer.

\textbf{Protocol and remediation.} Preserve methodology, software and configuration identities separately, data period and composition, inventory and scope, expected/actual components, executed and excluded counts, discrepancies, effects, corrective owner and retest. Distinguish rule conformity, data quality and decision-ground completeness. A defect record links affected versions, dates and applications, not just the complaint revealing it. A clarification record links the original receipt, source or competent recipient, legal period, reply, corrected calculation and notification. Protected records stay inside authorized storage; the public extract contains only disclosure-checked aggregates.

\section*{Launch conformity without a new entitlement transition}
Paragraph 26 now concerns repair, retesting and lawful software release management. It does not require a second manual decision service. A previous build may operate only if it executes the rule legally applicable to that decision and its controls and operational suitability are verified. A build carrying a superseded income rule is not a lawful fallback merely because it passed older tests. Outage measures remain confined to Resolution No. 35 annex 1 paragraph 48; other defects do not enlarge that power.

The submitted normative text contains no Presidential reserve preserving the 2026 method, inclusive-income entitlement or transition-debt exemption. Those choices need separate entitlement and fiscal evidence and are unnecessary to the narrow calculation-conformity contribution. The equality and land-floor alternatives below remain conditional research options, not requested conditions of adoption. Removing the reserve does not pretend that software can cure a missing legal rule: the developers must complete the lawful decision table and resolve any absence or conflict before first application. Under O‘RQ-682 Article 55, competent official interpretation may explain a subordinate rule but may not insert an amendment. Only the competent adopter may change the criterion or commencement. Existing awards, pending applications and recovery remain governed by their applicable legislation; this proposal invents no interim entitlement.

\section*{Scientific and technical part: calculation task}
This part is working technical material explaining the calculation requirements. The precise coefficients and boundaries of the 2027 multidimensional scoring model and real-family data have not yet been obtained. The completed audit of published calculation rules in current Resolution No. 35 is presented separately below. Symbols in the general formulas are not official criteria or category names.

In general, an approved rule is described as \(C=g(F(x),q,v)\), where \(x\) denotes relevant inputs, \(q\) quality and exception states, and \(v\) the version applied. \(F\) need not be linear. Ministries’ and agencies’ current refusal and assistance-award rules are accounted for separately.

Only if the rule is additive,
\[S=\sum_j w_jz_j.\]
For justified closed intervals \(z_j\in[a_j,b_j]\),
\[L=\sum_j\min(w_ja_j,w_jb_j),\qquad U=\sum_j\max(w_ja_j,w_jb_j).\]
This interval is exact when inputs can independently take any rational value within their respective intervals. For discrete or dependent inputs, it is a conservative outer bound that may include unattainable categories. Bounds must have a methodological or data basis. If the interval spans several categories, calculation uncertainty is recorded. This calculation grants no power to assign a value in place of unresolved data or to refuse assistance.

The working program uses \(C(S)=|\{t:t\leq S\}|\) for testing: equality belongs to the category with the higher index. This is only a test convention; equality, rounding, exceptions and direction in the official rule require separate approval.

Recalculation disagreement is
\[R=\frac{\text{number of category disagreements}}{\text{number of comparable cases}}.\]
For a sensitivity scenario,
\[F_s=\frac{\text{number of cases changing category}}{\text{scenario cases with both categories determined}}.\]
The report states the scenario, numerator, denominator, and excluded or unresolved cases separately. With a zero denominator, the measure is recorded as undefined.

The electronic computational supplement to Material 3 demonstrates an additive rule, exact rational arithmetic, closed intervals, boundary equality and comparison with a control category. It separately supplies the published-rule audit and independent arithmetic checks. The findings required to evaluate the proposed provisions are reproduced in this note; repository access is unnecessary.

\textbf{1. Reconciling category boundaries.} Annex paragraphs 5 and 12 include the equality case above as a mandatory test. The final policy and legal solution is determined together with the act of higher legal force. Replacing \(<\) with \(\leq\) through a technical setting is not proposed.

For discussion, if the option including equality in a principal category is selected, the income condition “\textbf{less than minimum consumption expenditure}” in PF-258 paragraph 3(a) and (b) and Resolution No. 35 annex 1 paragraph 39(a) and (b) is reconciled as “\textbf{not exceeding minimum consumption expenditure}”. The condition “\textbf{when below minimum consumption expenditure}” in PF-258 annex 1 note (a) is likewise changed to “\textbf{when not exceeding minimum consumption expenditure}”.

PF-258 paragraph 3(v) and Resolution No. 35 annex 1 paragraph 39(v) distinguish two income routes. Proposed criterion wording for discussion:
\begin{quote}
“The ‘family at the poverty boundary’ category shall distinguish:

families removed from the ‘state-supported family’ or ‘poor family’ category whose total average monthly income per family member, calculated without the hardship reduction coefficient, is above minimum consumption expenditure but does not exceed one and a half times that amount;

families included on the recommendation of the ‘mahalla seven’ under the separate procedure for families in difficult life circumstances. In this route, income after the 25 per cent reduction must not exceed one and a half times minimum consumption expenditure; reduced income equal to or below minimum consumption expenditure shall not itself constitute grounds for refusing inclusion. Statutory requirements concerning qualifying family circumstances, initial assessment, recommendation, the prescribed quota and reassessment are preserved.”
\end{quote}
The second route is reconciled with PF-258 annex 1 note (b) and Resolution No. 35 annex 1 paragraphs 34–35. Ordinary income and income reduced by 25 per cent are not compared as the same value. This author’s option requires the developer’s assessment of entitlement and category effects, amendment of the Presidential act and reconciliation of other relevant rules; amendment of the Cabinet of Ministers resolution alone is insufficient. If another lawful equality solution is selected, its category, refusal and exception conditions must also be stated openly and consistently.

\textbf{Additional control case after review, outside the original case list:} let a constructed three-person single-parent family satisfying lawful recommendation and existing quota conditions, with no other refusal ground, have three-month income of 8\,580\,000 soum. Ordinary income is \(8\,580\,000/3/3=2\,860\,000/3>715\,000\); reduced income is \((2\,860\,000/3)\times3/4=715\,000\). Under paragraph 35(b), this reduced value does not exceed the upper bound. A universal lower bound \(>P\) for all routes is therefore not used because it conflicts with this case. The two-route wording above addresses that problem. This control checks legal conditions; it does not describe an observed family’s outcome.

\textbf{2. Preventing negative area in land calculations.} The area formula in Resolution No. 35 annex 1 paragraph 27 is proposed as:
\begin{quote}
“\(TEM=\max\{0;UM-QM-\min(0.2UM;200\text{ m²})\}\).

Here \(\min\) selects the smallest and \(\max\) the largest value in its brackets. \(TEM\) shall not be negative. \(UM\) and \(QM\) shall use the same unit. If data on either are unavailable or \(QM>UM\), the data shall be clarified at the authoritative source; unknown or contradictory data shall not be replaced by zero. Clarification shall not introduce a new refusal ground based on that circumstance alone.”
\end{quote}
This is a proposed normative amendment; it has not been confirmed that existing software already contains this floor. Obtaining the land-tax coefficient and other normative-income conditions are preserved. Application date, recalculation and effects on earlier decisions will be determined in the final act.

\textbf{3. Clarifying periods and versions.} Annex paragraphs 4, 6 and 8 require recording whether each value is monthly or a period total, the relevant calendar months and the parameter in force in those months. A complete migration calculation rule has not been implemented until the period for comparing a remittance with \(2M\) in Resolution No. 35 paragraph 26 is clarified. Paragraph 19’s rule applying the exchange rate on the date YAMIH receives the information requires preservation of that date and rate for replay.

The extended audit calculates two \textbf{conditional} readings separately; neither is presented as the Agency’s operational algorithm. If compared \(q\) is monthly, for listed countries the move from \(q=2M-1\) to \(q=2M\) lowers the monthly normative contribution from \(3M\) to \(2M\), a decrease of 1\,360\,000 soum. If \(q\) is interpreted as a three-month total, the same conditional change is from \(3M\) to \(2M/3\), a decrease of \(9\,520\,000/3\) soum. For other countries the corresponding conditional decreases are \(0\) and \(5\,440\,000/3\) soum. These are not assistance amounts. The developer must explicitly determine and legally and scientifically justify the period and transition from a normative to an actual value at the boundary; a programmer does not independently introduce an additional minimum-income floor.

A zero floor in the land formula may increase a negative normative contribution. For September’s \(M\) and test assumption \(k=1\), the maximum change in the monthly household-income contribution in the declared algebraic domain is 95\,200 soum; the per-person contribution divides this by the number of family members. The proposal is not interpreted as automatically increasing assistance. Effects on current categories, payments and earlier decisions require separate replay using lawful data.

These results do not justify abolishing coefficients, reducing assistance or concluding that a particular percentage of families will change category. Such findings require the official model, lawful data in an authorized environment and a prespecified empirical examination.

\textbf{Remedy for an unlawful refusal.} Appeals-law Articles 32–33 provide a challenge to unlawful refusal to accept or consider and explanation of the review procedure; \href{https://lex.uz/docs/-6445145}{Constitution Article 55} protects judicial review of unlawful decisions, actions and inaction. Articles 185–189 of the \href{https://lex.uz/docs/-3527353}{Administrative Court Proceedings Code} provide the applicable framework. Article 189 requires both illegality and infringement of the applicant’s protected rights or interests for the stated remedy; the court may require a lawful decision, action or other cure. A reasoned policy disagreement alone is not proof of that infringement. Article 186 sets a general six-month court-application period from learning of the violation, unless another statutory period applies; restoration requires a valid reason. This is distinct from the one-year period for a complaint to a superior body under appeals-law Article 22. An actual challenge requires the facts, classification, competent court and current rules to be checked at that time. No judgment ordering adoption of this proposal has been obtained or identified.

\textbf{Scope of the legal conclusion.} O‘RQ-682 Article 23 makes discussion proposals advisory but requires consideration; Article 24 expressly permits rejection with reasons. The legal assurance supported here is the existence of the specified procedural duties, with their statutory conditions and remedies. It is not an assurance of appointment, access to restricted material, incorporation or adoption. Article 3 of \href{https://lex.uz/docs/-3492199}{O‘RQ-457} excludes preparation and adoption of normative acts from its scope. Therefore the individual administrative-decision requirements cited elsewhere in this package are not presented as the procedural basis for compelling enactment; the proposed appeals-law route rests on the citizen’s submission and the distinct constitutional and statutory duties described above.

\textbf{Applicable procedure and judicial proof.} Appeals-law Article 1 excludes submissions whose consideration procedure is established by other legislation. This covering letter is prepared as an unsolicited citizen proposal and personal request, not as a completed formal discussion submission. If the authority applies a special procedure, the requested reply should identify that procedure and its legal basis; the ordinary proposal deadline is not asserted for a subsequent portal comment under O‘RQ-682 Article 24. No binding decision settling the classification of this mixed letter has been identified. This is an interpretation limit, not evidence that the author lacks the constitutional right to submit a proposal.

For a challenge within the applicable administrative-court procedure, \href{https://lex.uz/docs/-3527353}{Code Article 67} places the proof burden on the challenged authority or official, requires confirmation of facts relied on for objections and limits them to the grounds on which they relied in deciding or acting. The applicant still participates in collecting evidence within their capacity and must prove any claimed amount of damage. \href{https://lex.uz/docs/-4711311}{Supreme Court Plenum Resolution No. 24, paragraphs 17 and 20}, explains the legality burden and states that the court does not assess the expediency of a decision or action lawfully left to administrative discretion. Paragraph 21 specifically explains the remedy for a written appeal not considered within the prescribed period: the court declares the inaction unlawful and orders consideration within a court-set period without predetermining the decision to be made. These rules support scrutiny of the actual grounds for refusal and compliance with applicable duties; they do not establish automatic success or a judicial duty to select the author’s policy.
